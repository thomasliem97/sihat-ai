01

In the running product

These models run when someone uses SihatAI.

  • MedGemma 1.5 4B-IT on Modal, via FastAPI: imaging, laboratory and clinical documents, structured findings, report drafts, and Ask the scan answers.
  • MY-LoRA (QLoRA adapter, PEFT at serve time): patient Ask the scan phrasing only. Analysis runs with the adapter off.
  • MedASR, with Whisper fallback: speech-to-text for voice triage. Replies label the active engine.
  • OpenAI embeddings, when the query and chunk share the same model: dense ranking on top of BM25 over MOH Quick Reference chunks.
  • Server MPEG text-to-speech: cached spoken replay of a triage reply.
02

Code generation

Cursor AI, including GPT-5.6 Sol, assisted implementation across Laravel, Vue, FastAPI, tests, and glue code: services, jobs, policies, routes, components, and Modal inference wiring.

The team directed the architecture, reviewed diffs, ran tests, and accepted or rejected generated code. AI did not merge, deploy, or approve production behaviour on its own.

03

Data analysis / model training

MY-LoRA was trained with QLoRA on Modal from curated Malaysian guideline and communication examples. The team prepared splits, launched training, shipped adapter weights, and chose when the adapter is loaded (patient Ask the scan) versus left off (analysis).

No identifiable patient data was used for training. Fixtures used in development are open, licensed, or labelled synthetic.

04

Writing & documentation

Cursor AI assisted README copy, this disclosure, the project summary, the technical architecture manuscript, interface microcopy, and pitch wording.

The team edited claims against the repository, chose what to assert, and remains the author of record. Generative drafts were not published without human review.

05

Design / visual assets

Cursor AI assisted iteration of the Clinical Field Atlas UI, the pitch-deck HTML, landing-page layout, and related visual structure (type, spacing, specimen-sheet composition).

Brand direction, palette, and final visual decisions were made by the team. AI did not replace a designer of record for clinical meaning, status colour, or accessibility choices.

06

Human review

AI accelerated coding, training, writing, and visual iteration. It did not own product decisions, clinical claims, or release. The SihatAI team reviewed, tested, and approved the work listed above.

SihatAI is decision support, not autonomous diagnosis. Product model outputs still require a clinician to review the original artifact.
