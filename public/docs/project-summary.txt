SihatAI is an end-to-end multimodal clinical intelligence platform for Malaysia's healthcare ecosystem. It addresses rising diagnostic demand, specialist shortages, fragmented records, language barriers, and patients' difficulty understanding complex medical information. Two roles, physician and patient, share three pillars: multimodal record analysis, Ask the scan, and voice triage. The platform turns medical images, laboratory and clinical documents, historical records, and spoken symptoms into structured, evidence-grounded insights in English, Bahasa Melayu, Mandarin, and Tamil.

Live inference uses MedGemma 1.5 on Modal through FastAPI. A Malaysia-focused MY-LoRA adapter is trained with QLoRA and loaded at serve time through PEFT. Analysis runs with the adapter off. Patient Ask the scan may load it for CPG and Bahasa phrasing. Laravel de-identifies a sibling file, then routes by filename, MIME, and DICOM tags. Supported inputs include X-rays, dermatology, ophthalmology, clinical documents, CT and MRI as up to eight separate mid-volume slices (overlay shows slice 0), histopathology as 3x3 center patches, and laboratory PDFs.

Every submission follows a seven-stage pipeline: upload, sibling PII de-identification, modality routing, MedGemma analysis with imaging and document specialist hops, guideline retrieval, ALLOW/WARN clinical guardrails, and report composition. The orchestrator extracts structured findings and biomarkers, localizes visual findings with prompted bounding boxes, applies heuristic confidence gating, and compares results with prior studies. Critical cases write durable audit events and notify the clinician.

To reduce unsupported claims, SihatAI retrieves over Malaysian Ministry of Health Quick Reference chunks. BM25 always runs. Dense ranking is added only when query and chunk embeddings share the same OpenAI model, then maximal marginal relevance. Findings carry source, section, excerpt, and relevance when retrieval is strong. Weak retrieval returns empty citations rather than stubs. The safety layer applies low-confidence abstention, WARN veto of patient prose, critical-value escalation, and physician sign-gated patient release. Role-based authorization and HMAC-signed webhooks protect health data.

One findings object generates two audience-specific reports. Physicians receive technical summaries, confidence-scored differentials, visual annotations, recommendations, and citations, then edit and sign the draft. Patients receive plain-language explanations, action plans, and questions for their doctor in their preferred language, only when policy allows and the physician has signed. Ask the scan answers questions about the study already on the page: click a finding or overlay box; Laravel abstains outside the field of view; replies stream as hops and tokens. Voice triage uses MedASR with Whisper fallback and server MPEG speech to convert spoken or typed symptoms into routine, urgent, or emergency classifications.

The stack is decoupled: Python and FastAPI power the AI microservice; PHP 8.4 and Laravel 13 manage records, database queues, webhooks, policies, and Fortify authentication; Vue 3, TypeScript, Inertia.js 3, Tailwind CSS 4, and Vite deliver the apps. Quality is backed by Pest 4, Larastan, and ESLint.

SihatAI is a safety-engineered clinical workflow. It helps clinicians review faster, keeps AI outputs auditable, and gives Malaysians clearer access to their health information without replacing clinical judgment.
