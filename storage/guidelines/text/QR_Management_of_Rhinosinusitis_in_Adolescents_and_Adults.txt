1. Rhinosinusitis is a common health problem characterised by mucosal 
inflammation of the nose & paranasal sinuses.
2. Important risk factors for rhinosinusitis are active & passive smoking, family 
history, asthma & gastroesophageal reflux disease.
3. In acute rhinosinusitis (ARS), the duration of symptoms is <12 weeks. 
4. Majority of ARS cases are viral in origin, with only 0.5 - 2.0% are 
complicated by bacterial infection.
5. Anterior rhinoscopy should be performed as part of clinical assessment of 
suspected ARS in primary care setting.
6. Plain radiography has no role in the routine management of rhinosinusitis.
7. Endoscopically-directed middle meatal culture should be used in 
diagnosing acute bacterial rhinosinusitis (ABRS) & chronic rhinosinusitis 
(CRS), instead of nasal swab culture.
8. Intranasal corticosteroids & nasal saline irrigation are the mainstay 
treatment of rhinosinusitis.
9. Antibiotic should be considered in patients with severe ARS.
10. Surgery is indicated for ARS with orbital or intracranial complications & 
CRS not responding to optimal medical therapy.
